Privacy Policy

PDF Reader

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Your Privacy First: PDF Reader works locally on your device. We do NOT collect, transmit, or store your personal files or document contents on any remote servers.

1. Introduction

Welcome to PDF Reader ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles information when you use our mobile application published on the Amazon Appstore and Google Play Store.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Device Storage & Document Access:

  • Our application requires permission to access device storage (including READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, and MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) solely to discover, open, read, convert, and organize your local document files (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Text, CSV, RTF) on your device.
  • No File Uploads: All file processing (reading, indexing, converting images to PDF, merging PDFs) occurs 100% locally on your device. Your documents are never uploaded to any external server or shared with third parties.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Our application is supported by advertisements to remain free for all users. We integrate verified ad networks:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to serve banner, interstitial, and native ad experiences. AdMob may collect anonymous device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) for ad delivery and analytics according to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Security

We implement strict local storage security practices. Since all document data remains on your device, your files are protected by your device's built-in Android operating system security features.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.
