Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 1, 2026 | App: Battery Monitor

Welcome to Battery Monitor ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed by our Android application.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application (Battery Monitor, package name com.kabihunterx42.batterymonitor).

1. Information We Collect & How It Is Used

Battery Monitor is designed with privacy first. We do not collect, store, or transmit any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location to any private servers.

  • Battery & Device Vitals: The app monitors battery level, charging status, voltage, temperature, and charging history. All this data remains 100% strictly local on your device.
  • App Settings & Preferences: Your alarm thresholds, notification configurations, and display preferences are stored locally on your device via Android DataStore / Room database.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our app uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, native, and rewarded advertisements. AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous data to serve personalized or contextual ads in compliance with Google's policies.

Data collected by Google AdMob includes:

  • Advertising ID (GAID) or device identifiers
  • IP address and broad location estimates
  • App interaction and ad performance metrics

For more details on how Google uses and protects data, please review:

Google Privacy & Terms Policy

How Google Uses Information From Apps That Use Their Services

3. Android Device Permissions

To provide real-time battery monitoring, our app requests the following system permissions:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used exclusively to show the persistent battery status notification and trigger alarm notifications.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Required to continuously read battery telemetry in the background when your device is charging or discharging.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required solely by the Google AdMob SDK to load and display advertisements.

4. Data Storage & Security

All battery telemetry, log entries, and alarm configurations created within the app are stored exclusively in local device storage. We do not maintain any cloud databases or external user accounts.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly target, solicit, or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided data through the app, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective steps.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Effective Date." You are advised to review this policy periodically for any changes.
