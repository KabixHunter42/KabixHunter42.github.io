Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Music Player ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application.

Core Privacy Guarantee: Music Player is built primarily as an offline music player. Your local audio files, playlists, and personal media stay on your device and are never uploaded to external servers.

2. Device Permissions & Information Collection

To provide high-fidelity offline audio playback, equalizer effects, lyrics, tag editing, and ringtone creation, our application requests access to certain device features:

  • Storage & Audio Access Permissions (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used exclusively to scan, index, and play local audio files stored on your device memory or SD card.
  • Write Storage Access (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / Audio Media Store): Required only when you use the built-in Tag Editor or Ringtone Cutter to save updated song metadata or new custom ringtone files to your device.
  • System Settings Access (WRITE_SETTINGS): Optional permission requested only when you choose to set a custom trimmed audio file directly as your default phone Ringtone, Alarm, or Notification tone.

3. Advertising Services (Google AdMob)

Our application uses Google AdMob to display banner, native advanced, and interstitial advertisements to support ongoing app development. Google AdMob may collect and process device advertising identifiers and anonymized usage telemetry to serve relevant ads.

Google's privacy practices regarding advertising are governed by Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google AdMob uses data

4. Local Data Storage & Preferences

All your user settings, favorite songs, custom playlists, equalizer profiles, and lyrics configurations are stored locally on your device using Android Shared Preferences and local database storage.

We do not maintain external database servers or user accounts to track your listening history.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

6. User Choices & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data and device permissions:

  • Revoking Permissions: You can revoke device storage and audio permissions at any time via your Android Device Settings (Settings > Apps > Music Player > Permissions).
  • Clearing App Data: You can wipe all app preferences, cached lyrics, and custom playlists by clearing app storage (Settings > Apps > Music Player > Storage > Clear Data).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory compliance. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
