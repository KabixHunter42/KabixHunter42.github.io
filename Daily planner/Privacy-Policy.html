Privacy Policy

Welcome to Daily Planner. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Daily Planner and how we use it.

1. Information Collection and Use

Daily Planner is designed as a personal organization tool. We want you to know that:

  • Local Storage: All your personal data, including Notes, Tasks, Diary entries, Bookmarks, and Calendar events, are stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to your personal content, and we do not store it on any external servers.
  • No Account Required: You can use the app without creating an account or providing any personal identification information (email, name, etc.).

2. Third-Party Services (Advertising)

To keep the app free, we display advertisements using Google AdMob. This third-party service may collect certain information to provide personalized ads. This information may include:

  • Advertising ID (e.g., AAID)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Approximate location (city/country)

For more information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Security

Since all your data is stored locally, its security depends on your device's security. We recommend using the "App Lock" feature provided within the app settings to add an extra layer of protection to your data.

4. Children's Privacy

Daily Planner does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our app, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
