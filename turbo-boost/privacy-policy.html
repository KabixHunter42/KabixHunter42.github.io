Privacy Policy for Turbo Boost

Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to Turbo Boost. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Turbo Boost is designed to optimize device performance and enhance your gaming experience. We collect minimal data necessary for functionality:

  • Device Performance Telemetry: Real-time hardware telemetry such as RAM allocation, CPU thermal metrics, battery percentage, network ping latency, and display refresh rates are processed locally on your device to display system metrics.
  • Installed Games & Apps: Querying installed packages is used strictly to provide the Game Space launcher functionality. This data remains on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

2. Permissions Requested

Turbo Boost requests permissions solely to deliver core performance tools:

  • System Alert Window (Overlay): Used to render the optional floating performance HUD overlay.
  • Do Not Disturb (Access Notification Policy): Used to suppress notifications during active boosted gaming sessions.
  • Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used to track gameplay session durations locally.

3. Advertising & Analytics

Turbo Boost uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), IP addresses, and diagnostic information to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in compliance with Google Play policies.

For more information on how Google manages advertising data, please review Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.

4. Data Security

We prioritize security. All performance monitoring data is processed locally on your device. We do not store or transmit personal identifiers to third-party servers.

5. Children's Privacy

Turbo Boost is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
